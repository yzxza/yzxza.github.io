知情同意书智能撰写项目复盘Turning SOP Templates into Rules AI Can Execute: A Retrospective on AI-Drafted Informed Consent Forms

用大模型写受控医学文档，难点不在"写得通顺"，而在"错了能不能被发现"。这篇记录我在一个知情同意书（ICF）智能撰写项目里的思考：为什么要先治理模板、系统怎么设计、输出怎么让人核对，以及我踩过的坑。When LLMs draft controlled medical documents, the hard part is not fluent writing but whether mistakes can be caught. This retrospective covers an AI project for drafting informed consent forms (ICFs): why the template had to be governed first, how the system was designed, how the output was made easy to review, and the pitfalls along the way.
